Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO) in India (2025)

Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO) is when a company transfers all or part of its recruitment activities to an external provider. Unlike traditional staffing, RPO is a strategic partnership where the agency becomes an extension of your HR team. RPO in India has grown 35% annually as companies realize the cost and quality benefits.

What is RPO and How Does It Work?

In an RPO model, we embed our recruiters within your organization (physically or virtually). We manage your entire recruitment process: job posting, screening, interviewing, offer management, and onboarding. You get a dedicated team that knows your company inside and out, delivering candidates who are perfectly matched to your needs.

Types of RPO Models

  • Full RPO: We manage your entire recruitment function. Best for companies hiring 50+ positions per month.
  • Project RPO: We handle hiring for a specific project or expansion. Ideal for new office setups or product launches.
  • Selective RPO: We manage specific roles or departments. Good for companies with strong internal teams but niche hiring needs.
  • On-Demand RPO: Scalable recruitment support during peak hiring periods. Pay only for what you use.

RPO Pricing in India

RPO pricing varies by model and volume. Full RPO typically costs Rs. 5,000-15,000 per hire for IT positions and Rs. 2,000-5,000 for non-IT roles. For companies hiring 100+ positions per month, per-hire costs can drop below Rs. 3,000. This is significantly cheaper than traditional agency fees of 8.33-15% of CTC.

Benefits of RPO

Companies that switch to RPO typically see: 40% reduction in cost-per-hire, 60% reduction in time-to-hire, 30% improvement in quality of hire, and 50% reduction in HR team workload. RPO also provides scalability, compliance management, and employer branding support.

Why Choose Us for RPO

We don't just fill positions - we optimize your entire hiring ecosystem for better results and lower costs.
